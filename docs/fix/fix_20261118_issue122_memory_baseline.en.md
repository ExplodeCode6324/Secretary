## 4. Evidence and scoring contract

1. **Actual request and access:** capture physical provider payloads with role/call attribution and hashes, as in `live-model.ts`/`evidence-audit.ts`. For must-retrieve, prove relevant facts and semantic echoes absent from Context, resident role profile, Consciousness and automatic recall at the eligible request. Opaque/encrypted provider state, oversized uninspected payload or unavailable semantic absence evidence yields `UNRESOLVED`. Distinguish automatic recall, explicit tool call, tool result, cited reference and final use. A correct unsupported guess cannot pass a must-retrieve case. Absence assessment uses hidden fact IDs and semantic inspection, not only exact substring checks.
2. **Transitions:** record A/B source acceptance and handling, maintenance revisions, authorization decisions, operation/World commit sequences and request-freeze boundaries. Do not demand changes to already-frozen in-flight requests. For a committed correction, evaluate the next request eligible to see that commit; failure to produce the commit is a separate extraction/authorization/maintenance finding. Do not silently wait forever or skip the first eligible request.
3. **M3:** separately report (a) target bytes/share and survival across maintenance, (b) provenance/authority chain and whether evidence/activity was invented, (c) stable item IDs and merge/split lineage. No stable IDs means layer (c) NOT_TESTED, not a pass; (a) cannot substitute for (b). Host job completion proves mechanics only. At least two ordinary maintenance cycles are required by C08/C09/C16; preserve failed attempts and retry lineage.
4. **M5 and effects:** keep the hidden obligation oracle independent of SUT state. Map Wait/Act/Satisfied/Invalidated onto existing input/Task/execution/operation records without adding product states. Exercise only owned local files; independently count actual writes and verify hashes, identity and durable journal. Require pre-fault operation binding, post-fsync marker, old-process exit proof, new-process identity, UNKNOWN state, verify-write response and subsequent same-identity history. A host-triggered verify-write proves the ordinary reconciliation API, not autonomous model reconciliation. Unsupported retired revival is a capability gap. Partial effect observation never becomes universal no-effect proof.
5. **Probe contamination:** closed-loop probes intentionally join subsequent history and are marked. Independent current/history/control/oracle-reader probes use separate #120 checkpoint branches from the same stopped, verified source. Restored branches share lineage and are not independent samples. Oracle-reader and fault-injection results stay diagnostic, outside natural-run results.
6. **Verdicts:** use separate host prerequisite/effect outcomes and semantic PASS/FAIL/UNRESOLVED, with NOT_TESTED/UNSUPPORTED/DEFERRED_TIME and infrastructure causes kept explicit. Independent effect evidence prevails over contrary judge prose. The existing six-assertion frozen v3 protocol is reused unchanged for historical rows only. New #122 subcase assertions and calibration are an explicitly versioned extension, never retroactive relabeling. Before new judging, positive and negative controls must exercise stale-current versus legitimate history, authority promotion versus faithful uncertainty, and unsupported effects versus observed effects. Calibrate the extension with a bounded pilot; do not call static exposed controls a held-out validation set. No overall model rank, universal authority PASS, release threshold or mandatory Luna pass.
