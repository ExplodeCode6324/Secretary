## 6. State model and Master's confirmed decisions (R1)

Keep two dimensions: interaction display (stable/hover/pressed/focus/disabled) and business state (idle/pending/succeeded/failed/offline/stale). Not every component needs every business state. Pending must not disable all reading/navigation; disabled controls do not animate. Actual receipts and text express outcomes; decoration and restored geometry do not indicate completion. Cancelling decoration does not cancel submitted work; business cancellation requires its own action and receipt. All colors, including status colors, follow v1.0.

### 6.1 Window layout, surfaces and separators

- Use the v1.0 web layout as the proportional reference. As the window changes width, **the central conversation grows or shrinks while both sidebars keep fixed widths within the same layout breakpoint**. Do not stretch the sidebars proportionally with the window. Narrow windows may use v1.0 breakpoints to select fixed widths, collapse or overlay; distinguish breakpoint changes from continuous stretching.
- Separate the sidebars from the center through background surfaces, without card containers. Every selected left-navigation item adopts the central surface color and connects at the boundary, appearing to merge into the center.
- Prefer separators reaching the edges of their owning column/module. Text padding should not shorten structural rules. Minimize inset decorative rules and the modern card-style appearance; edge-to-edge does not mean crossing unrelated columns.
- Verify continuous resizing and breakpoint changes: width differences go to the center, sidebars stay stable, selected rows join the central surface, and structural rules align. Consult effective v1.0 CSS and its actual UI for dimensions and colors.

### 6.2 Three-frame coverage and sidebar experiments

- Apply three-frame motion to all interactive buttons, text inputs, Master and assistant avatars, and existing structural separators. Use v1.0’s effect appropriate to each object rather than applying one icon effect everywhere. Inputs animate boundaries/display layers; body text, input text, caret and IME candidate position remain stable. Avatars retain image semantics.
- Buttons respond to contact, press/release and focus, merging the same contact. Preserve disabled, reduced-motion, motion-off and cleanup behavior. Actions never wait for animation completion.
- **Separators trigger randomly, not on every eligible approach or interaction.** Add a random play/skip decision to v1.0’s event-driven mechanism, retaining randomized segments, cooldown and at most one active separator effect across the page. Do not add an autonomous background loop. Random segment selection in the original implementation is distinct from randomly deciding whether to play; document and implement that distinction. Tune and record the probability during the experiment rather than inventing an approved value in this plan.
- In #62’s component gallery, try the same three-frame language on **left-sidebar text** and **existing separators between its buttons**. Text effects change only temporary display layers, preserving layout, hit targets and accessible names. Separators remain edge-to-edge and randomly triggered; do not add unnecessary lines just to exhibit motion.
- Compare v1.0 baseline coverage against the sidebar extension for appearance, reading interference and performance, then show Master. This is an explicitly requested experiment, not an accepted replacement for the frozen reference. Preserve the v1.0 tag. This turn revises plans only; #61 documents the specification and handoff, while #62 implements the component experiment.
